## 2. Tile geometry [MEASURED]

- Every one of the 1,124 hub tiles rendered at the same box, 194 x 272 CSS px (column `tile_w`, `tile_h`), at a 1920 x 945 viewport. [CALC: 194 / 272 = 0.713, about 5:7 portrait]
- The served image files are almost all about 0.71: 1,021 of 1,124 round to 0.71 (366x512), 34 to 0.67 (341x512), 24 to 0.72, 18 to 0.75 (278x378 and similar), 14 to 0.56 (288x512), and 2 are square 512x512 (Find the Alien, Mechangelion). [MEASURED: `thumb_nat_w`/`thumb_nat_h` columns, parsed with a CSV parser, 1,124 rows]
- The tile image uses aspect-fill (`ytCoreImageContentModeScaleAspectFill` on the `<img>`, read in the DOM). A square image is therefore cropped to its centre. [CALC: 194 / 272 = 71% of a square image's width stays visible.] So anything important in a square upload must sit in the middle 71%.

## 3. Patterns

All counts are [CALC] over the 55 rows above (T = 40, P = 15).

Shared by the top 40 and the draw peers:
- Portrait 5:7, flat saturated colour, almost no photographic detail (Solar Smash is the one photoreal tile in the 55).
- Lettering is common: 26 of 40 top tiles (65%) and 11 of 15 peers (73%) have baked-in lettering, mostly the game's own name.
- Few subjects: 1 or 2 focal subjects in 24 of 40 top tiles (12 + 12) and in 13 of 15 peers (9 + 4).

Where the top 40 and the draw peers differ (all of this is correlation in a hand-made sample, none of it shows cause):

What that means, with the confound stated:
- The 15 peers were chosen because the title says draw, rescue or escape, and most are newer than the top 40. What they show is the current draw-genre convention: a distressed animal or person (crying kitten, terrified fish, frozen puppy, panicking dog, angry mother with a pan), a visible threat (bees in 2 peer tiles and in Save The Pets, a shark, a dragon, lava, a frying pan) and a hand-drawn illustration style, often with a pencil or drawing hand (Draw Bridge, Single Line, Draw Puzzle, Draw To Smash, Happy Glass). Save The Pets (rank 26) is the earlier example of that look in the top 40; whether the peers copy it is INFERRED from the look, not known. Both the peers at ranks 44 to 139 (10m to 26m plays) and most of those at ranks 211 to 676 (378k to 6.1m) carry the face and threat, so it does not separate the more-played peers from the less-played ones. UNPROVEN that the face or the threat is what earns plays.
- The six draw games inside the top 40 do not follow the peer convention: 4 are illustration or doodle, 2 are gameplay screens, and only 1 (Save The Pets) has a clear threat and a readable face. Thief Puzzle (rank 7) has a grin and a barking dog. So the biggest draw tiles are cartoon-doodle first, threat second.
- The top of the whole table is mostly not faces. Ranks 1 to 12 are 3D renders of an object or a faceless figure (a jelly runner, a ball, a roll of dessert, a phone case). A face with an emotion shows up in 11 of 40 top tiles, in 12 of 15 peers.

## 4. Logos and branding

- The rule as returned by WebFetch for the design page, section 4 (Metadata): the developer MUST NOT include any branding or logos in the thumbnails, description or title. [DOC https://developers.google.com/youtube/gaming/playables/certification/requirements_design, fetched 2026-11-05; the page text came back through WebFetch's summariser, so the exact wording is its extract, not a quote I read raw.] The same page says thumbnails come "in several different aspect ratios" and points to the Developer Portal for details (not fetched).
- Practice on the hub: 37 of the 55 tiles above carry lettering (26 + 11), almost always the game's own title as a styled wordmark. Examples with a lettered wordmark that is plainly a title logo: Build a Queen, Snake Clash, Stealth Master, Race Master 3D, Hole.io, Aquapark.io, Vehicle Masters, Fork & Sausage, DOP 2 and 4, Home Rush, Single Line Drawing Puzzle, Tricky Twist, Doge Rescue, Draw Puzzle, Happy Glass.
- Tiles whose lettering looks like a third-party or franchise mark, which is the riskier kind: Hill Climb Racing Lite (the franchise logo), Save Nemo (a famous film character's name), the .io names (Hole.io, Aquapark.io, State.io), and Girl Rescue: Dragon Out! (a fake "PLAY NOW! START CHALLENGE" button baked into the image). Mini Relaxing Game- pop it shows "ANTISTRESS GAMES", which is not its title.
- Whether a title wordmark counts as a "logo" under that rule is UNVERIFIED. All 37 are live on the hub, which shows only that they were accepted at some point, not that they would pass today.

## 5. What a BYC tile should and should not do

These are INFERRED from the table and the geometry. None is tested; no tile has been A/B tested by anyone here.

Should:
- Be built for 5:7 portrait. If the source art is square, keep everything that matters inside the middle 71% of the width. [CALC above]
- Show one subject doing one thing, with the problem readable at a glance: Milo (a yellow smiley stick figure, per the opening frame I saw at http://localhost:8080 on 2026-11-05) under or beside the red boulder, mid-fall. The peer pattern says a readable worried face plus a visible danger is what draw-rescue tiles use now (12 of 15 faces, 10 of 15 threats). That is the genre convention, not proof it works.
- Show the verb: one pencil or one drawing hand, or a half-drawn line. 5 of the 15 peers show one (Draw Bridge, Single Line, Draw Puzzle, Draw To Smash, Happy Glass), and so do top-40 tiles for DOP 2 and 4, Color Page, Phone Case DIY and Drawing Carnival. Note that several peers show the line already drawn and working (Draw Bridge: the car sits on the arc; Happy Glass: the water stream reaches the glass; Single Line: the outline is being traced), so the tile shows the satisfying outcome, not only the problem. Which of the two works better is not measured.
- Use flat, saturated, high-contrast colour against a plain background. BYC's own look (grey-white graph paper, thin black outline, yellow, red, green) is lower in saturation than almost every tile above, so give the tile a single strong accent (the red boulder, or a coloured background panel) and test it at the 194 x 272 size.

Should not:
- Carry any studio, platform or YouTube mark; keep it free of Playgama and YouTube marks regardless of how the title-wordmark question resolves. [DOC above] BYC's title, "BRO, YOU'RE COOKED.", names no subject and no action; baking it in as lettering would spend the tile's words on a joke, and the hub already prints the title under the tile. (INFERRED: no measurement of a vague title.)
- Use 4 or more words of lettering. Only 5 of 40 top tiles and 6 of 15 peers do, and the draw peers that do so (Draw Bridge, Home Rush, Single Line, Draw Puzzle, Happy Glass) are the ones that repeat their title.
- Copy a fake button (the "PLAY NOW! START CHALLENGE" button seen once, rank 493) or in-game UI that the platform tile will sit next to.
