# Cover design brief for BYC (9 Nov 2026): the rules a cover must pass before the owner sees it

Built from `REPORT.md`. Every rule has a check that can be done on the finished image. A concept that fails a check is fixed or dropped, not shown.

## The job
In one second, at 194 px (YouTube portrait) or 221 px (Playgama landscape) wide, a stranger must be able to say: "a little guy is about to get crushed, and I draw a line to save him."

## Formats, in order of importance
1. LANDSCAPE 1920x1080 (Playgama card, the draft's card image). Compose for 16:9; check at 221x124 and 448x252.
2. PORTRAIT 1080x1920 (YouTube hub 5:7, Playgama vertical). Check at 194x272.
3. SQUARE 800x800 (Playgama icon, shown about 123 px wide; the YouTube hub crops a square to its middle 71%). Check at 123x123 and at the 71% crop.

## Rules and checks
1. ONE idea, three elements at most: Milo, the boulder, the drawn line (plus the pencil as the verb). Check: cover the title; count distinct objects at thumbnail size; no more than three plus background.
2. SHOW THE VERB: the line is the loudest stroke in the picture (thickest, highest-contrast hue, white halo) and is visibly anchored to something solid, because in the game a line floating in mid-air falls. Check: a stranger points at the line first or second.
3. SHOW THE STAKES IN ONE FRAME: the boulder at least a quarter of the picture area, clearly above Milo, the gap readable; Milo's whole body visible, his face alarmed (the genre's convention: a face on 17 of 18 Playgama draw tiles). Check: Milo's head is at least one fifth of the tile height at 194x272.
4. BRIGHT, CLEAN, SEPARATED: a saturated background with the subject separating from it by luminance; no pale-paper look (the paper set sits at the 99th percentile for pale pixels); calm, not busy (top tiles have lower edge density than the tail); no graph-grid noise behind the subject. Check: greyscale version still reads; compare in a mock row of real neighbours.
5. LETTERING (owner approved 9 Nov): the title large, thick, outlined, short, in the top third or left side; at 221 px wide the capital height is at least 20 px. Make a lettered and a text-free version of the lead concept (the YouTube 'no logos' rule and a title wordmark is UNVERIFIED). No instructions, no 'PLAY NOW', no fake buttons, no slogans beyond the title.
6. HONEST: made from the game's own renderer and palette (graphite ink, blue for the player's line, red for danger only); no generated imagery; no real person, no other brand; the picture is a state the game can actually show.
7. SAFE ZONES: square content inside the middle 71% of the width; portrait content inside the central 5:7 band; title never within 6% of an edge.
8. DIFFERENT FROM ITS NEIGHBOURS AND FROM THE NEAR-TWIN: it must not look like Save The Pets (cream, a dog in a pit, bees); the Milo-and-boulder, notebook-world identity is the difference.

## Process gate (nothing is uploaded before the owner picks)
Four concepts, each scored against the rules above and against ten benchmark tiles; a written verdict per concept on whether it is the best that can be made; at least two rounds of revision; then one contact sheet showing each concept at full size and inside a mock Playgama landscape row and a mock YouTube portrait row, opened in the owner's browser.

## Testing after launch (UNVERIFIED that the platforms offer a cover A/B)
Keep the runner-up. Swap one cover at a time, log the date, the cover id and the plays per day before and after; read it as a hint only, because time of week and traffic source confound it. Settle the covers before the free traffic boost (3 runs left).
